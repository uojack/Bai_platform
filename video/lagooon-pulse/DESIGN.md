# Design intent

Preserve the supplied AOS-Pulse-LAGOOON V1.1 screens as the visual source of truth. The television loop should feel like the native application is calmly breathing rather than like a slide deck.

- Home holds for 18 seconds.
- Network, Audio, Compute, Devices, and Space hold for 10 seconds each.
- The final 28 seconds return to Home so the complete 96-second environmental audio track loops without truncation.
- Transitions are restrained 0.8-second dissolves with a very small camera drift.
- No additional claims, widgets, or decorative UI are introduced.
